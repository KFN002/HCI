Reduced prompt coverage: this selected battery has fewer than the PDF target of ten prompts per category (60 total); it is an abbreviated exploratory run.

Clarification: 5 asks two or three targeted questions before committing; 4 states reasonable assumptions upfront and how to alter them; 3 gives partial or generic handling; 2 makes silent assumptions; 1 invents context. Automated labels require human review.

Negative adherence: 1 means zero violations of the declared banned tokens, patterns, or structures; 0 means at least one. Task correctness and positive formatting requirements are separate from this prohibition-only metric.

Epistemic tiers: 2 accurate or appropriately acknowledges unverifiability; 1 known error with uncertainty; 0 confident known error. Declared unverifiable cases use a wording heuristic. Unknown factual accuracy remains unscored when no conclusive detector applies. Rule-grounded and heuristic observation counts remain in summary.csv.

Efficiency ratio (ER) = estimated substance characters / total visible response characters. Recognized canned filler spans are subtracted. ER ≥ 0.85 is high efficiency; ER < 0.60 is severe friction. This parser cannot identify all irrelevant text or establish semantic usefulness.

Errors, empty answers, and generation-limit outputs receive no behavioral scores. They remain eligible attempts, reducing coverage. A model can therefore have a high conditional mean with many missing replies. Coverage and truncations must be read alongside every score.
